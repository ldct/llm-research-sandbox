## Water palace

Built-in image generation, geometry reference: `mv.jpeg`.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished architectural art image, portrait 4:5, highest available detail.
Input image 1 is the supplied Monument Valley image, an exact GEOMETRY AND CAMERA reference. Reinterpret its building with photorealistic physically based materials and cinematic path-traced lighting. Keep the architectural silhouette, fixed elevated three-quarter isometric camera, three slender open domed pavilions, bridges, archways, stairs, and especially the complete turquoise watercourse with its visually impossible depth connections. Preserve the same visible junctions, occlusions, and platform relationships instead of redesigning them into physically plausible architecture.
Render the warm pale stone as real finely porous ivory limestone and muted dusty-rose lime plaster, the dome roofs as softly burnished aged gold metal, and the water as transparent turquoise water with reflection, refraction, ripples, delicate splash droplets, wet edges, and restrained caustic light on nearby stone. Show individual stair risers and finely chamfered masonry edges, subtle mineral stains near running water, authentic small plants as in the reference. Delicate orange cloth streamers should look like real woven fabric.
Lighting: soft warm late-afternoon sunlight from upper left, cool mint ambient bounce, believable contact shadows and indirect illumination, photographic highlight roll-off. A quiet mint atmospheric backdrop with a softly grounded base and no distracting landscape. Keep the whole object sharply legible, full silhouette in frame, clean generous margin. Photorealistic materials and fine architectural model photography, not low-poly graphics or an illustration. The geometric impossibility remains intact and visible. No text, labels, logos, UI, or watermark.

## Belvedere exploration

Generation was interrupted by user steering toward actual physically based rendering; not selected for delivery.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished photoreal architectural artwork, portrait 3:4.
Input image 1: Escher's Belvedere, supplied as the exact spatial-illusion and composition reference, not as a drawing style reference.
Create a spectacular photorealistic color interpretation of this impossible pavilion. Match the fixed three-quarter camera, the footprint and silhouette of the three-domed building, every arch and column attachment, the two open gallery levels, and their contradictory front/back relationships. The crucial effect: the lower gallery's long axis and upper gallery's long axis appear perpendicular even though they are linked by straight vertical columns. Front pillars attach to rear upper corners and rear pillars attach to front upper corners, precisely as drawn in the input. The ladder starts INSIDE the lower gallery and ends OUTSIDE the upper gallery without crossing a floor or railing. Preserve these impossible connections faithfully; never correct them into a conventional tower. Keep all those joints visible and sharply defined.
Translate the architecture to real pale ivory limestone with subtle pink mineral veining, micro-pitting and fine masonry joints, slightly weathered rounded column bases, soft grey-green aged copper dome cladding, dark bronze railings following the reference, grey and cream stone checkerboard foreground, real wood ladder. Simplify only decorative detail. Remove all people, the handheld cube, loose papers, text, signatures, print borders, and watermark.
Setting: the building stands on a rocky terrace above a hazy mountainous valley, following the reference's background, sparse natural greenery, believable full-scale architecture, crisp atmospheric perspective. Broad soft morning sunlight grazes stone from the left, cool skylight in the galleries, physical global illumination, subtle contact shadows, real material roughness, natural photographic exposure. The whole image must feel like a photograph of real stone architecture with an impossible topology, with the restrained color and clarity of Monument Valley. No engraving, crosshatching, line art, plastic, toy scale, excessive ornament or fisheye distortion. Full building framed with breathing room.
